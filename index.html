01 — About

It's good to meet you!

I'm Josh Sinkinson, a Product Designer and Design Lead currently based in Aliso Viejo, CA.

Since July 2023, I've been leading design at Bluumly, an AI-powered SaaS attribution platform for Shopify merchants. As the sole designer, I've taken the product from 0 to 1 — architecting the entire UI/UX, establishing our brand identity, and building our marketing presence from the ground up.

I specialize in transforming complex technical requirements into intuitive interfaces. At Bluumly, this meant designing high-stakes workflows for AI-assisted content creation, conversational analytics, and social-attribution dashboards that make data-heavy processes feel effortless for users.

Beyond pushing pixels, I've built a comprehensive design system in Figma on shadcn/ui foundations that ensures 100% visual consistency across our platform, shipped a high-performance marketing site in Framer, and expanded into strategic leadership — facilitating cross-functional sprints and influencing product roadmap decisions to support early-stage growth.

03 — Brand & Visual Design

InBalance Soul

InBalance Soul reached out to give three of their room & body mists — Sacred Harmony, Positive Energy, and Balance & Relax — a modern facelift. As the sole designer on the project, I started with symbol exploration, digging into marks that could carry the brand's core message: balance, energy, and calm, distilled into a single icon per scent.
